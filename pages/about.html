Born on
Seymour Drive.

Bottomz Up Bar & Grill sits at 2001 Seymour Drive in South Boston, VA. A real neighborhood spot built around real food. Just a grill, a bar, and a crew that shows up every day to do it right.

The name says it all. We built a place where you can belly up to the bar, sink into a plate of Back Alley Burgers, and actually enjoy the night. Wings tossed in 10 house sauces from mild to Xtra Hot, cocktails poured straight, and a room that fills up with the kind of regulars who make a place worth coming back to.

Whether you're a SoBo local grabbing the Garbage Burger after a long shift, or a first-timer walking in on a Friday, you'll find the same menu, the same bar, the same welcome. Walk-ins always welcome.
